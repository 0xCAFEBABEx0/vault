## Abstract / Summary
Today we’re releasing Old Ideas, Novel Problems , a new study of how reliably large language models can evaluate the novelty of scientific research ideas. Alongside the paper, we’re publishing a benchmark, data, and code for testing novelty judges, plus an automated pipeline researchers can use to build new evaluation sets. At Ai2, we’ve been developing AI systems that support different parts of scientific discovery. DataVoyager helps researchers explore and analyze structured data, AutoDiscovery generates and tests hypotheses, and Theorizer synthesizes evidence across large bodies of literature into candidate scientific theories. Together with Asta , these projects point toward systems that can help researchers move from understanding prior work to proposing, testing, and refining new ideas. But generating research ideas is only part of the challenge of automating scientific discovery. Those ideas also need to contribute something genuinely new, which means these systems need a way to distinguish a novel direction from one that mostly repackages existing work.
