## Abstract / Summary
NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe is an open-weight, promptable model that turns recorded speech into useful text, now available on Hugging Face and ModelScope . It brings transcription, speaker attribution (diarization), translation, and audio question answering into one checkpoint: it transcribes 40 languages, translates 31 of them to or from English, and attributes speakers in 13. It handles recordings up to an hour long and labels up to eight speakers.
